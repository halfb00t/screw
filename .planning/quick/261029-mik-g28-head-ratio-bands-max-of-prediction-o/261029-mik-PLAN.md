## The rule (G28)

The ratios are bolt k/d and s/d, and nut s/d and m/d. Each ratio's band is [min, max] over the
fitted points of its kind. The fitted points are those R11 fits; read each one's params through the
parameter model, so defaults are filled in. The bands are generated by code and emitted into
`calc/cost.py` by `bench.oper_fit emit-constants`; no band is typed by hand (L02).

For a part with any ratio outside its band, each target is predicted:
- raw;
- with each out-of-band ratio set to edge x d (field k, s or m, everything else held):
  - above its band: both band edges (min and max), every combination;
  - below its band: its low edge only.

The prediction is the largest of these, target by target (rulings 4 and 5). Above the band this is
the largest prediction anywhere in the band. Below it, it is max(raw, low edge).

ASSUMPTION (ruling 5): a smaller head never costs more than one at the low edge. It is unmeasured.
07-15's below-band points test it. Admission (R12) then applies the envelope
to that prediction as today.

Inside every band the prediction is unchanged, bit for bit, so fit.json does not change.

07-15's runner checks read out-of-band points at 5 repetitions:
- per kind, artifact and ratio, at least one point above the band and one below it.

They are chosen from parts admitted under the rule. If any reading exceeds its enveloped
prediction, the phase stops and asks the owner. Such a reading does not widen `e` silently, because
widening could move B-01's admitted set.
