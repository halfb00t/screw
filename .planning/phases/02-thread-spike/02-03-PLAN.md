## Phase Goal

ROADMAP **Goal** (verbatim; not in user-story form — run `/gsd-mvp-phase 2` to restate it; no story
is invented here): The owner knows, from a pre-registered and recorded measurement rather than a
guess, which helical construction to build, which volume estimator to trust, what turn cap each
size needs, and whether a kernel pair proof can be made falsifiable, all before any field that
builds a thread exists.

<objective>

pre-registered rule, the full row record (rod and void, fine and preview meshes, STEP, gzip, the STL
check under a ceiling), guarded run blocks that stream JSONL under a validated run id, and the
kernel-free verdict rules that turn those records into the pass bar, the escape clause, the budget
caps, K, the estimator and the turn cap per size.

Purpose: questions 1, 2 and 4 (construction and frontier, mesh budget, estimator) become runnable and
judgeable by code written before any data exists.
Output: grid and frontier maths, four blocks, the `verdict` subcommand, tests at every threshold.
