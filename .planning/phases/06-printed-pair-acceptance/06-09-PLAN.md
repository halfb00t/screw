## Phase Goal

ROADMAP **Goal** (verbatim; not in user-story form, so no story is invented): The owner holds a bolt
and a nut printed from this tool at the default clearance that thread together by hand, and that
default and the printability threshold come from a recorded printed test, not from a forum or vendor
figure.

<objective>
INFO-04 and SC5: the threshold run 2 reads becomes two constants and a warning on both kinds and all
three front ends. Task 1 (tracer) adds the constants and the warning, wires both kinds, and proves it
end to end with the record re-deriving the constants. Task 2 logs the threshold and ticks INFO-04, and
carries the end-of-phase look at the page.

Purpose: sizes and pitches below what printed get an honest warning citing its source. Output: the
constants and builder, the two insertions, their tests, the decision entry, the requirement evidence.
</objective>
