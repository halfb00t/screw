## Phase Goal

ROADMAP **Goal** (verbatim; not in user-story form, so no story is invented): The owner holds a bolt
and a nut printed from this tool at the default clearance that thread together by hand, and that
default and the printability threshold come from a recorded printed test, not from a forum or vendor
figure.

<objective>
Run 1 and the bought-nut test, as pre-registered. Task 1 (tracer) exports every run-1 part through the
product's own path and pins the manifest. Task 2 is the owner's print and fit test, recorded in the two
landed records. Task 3 reads the records with the landed rules, pins them and writes the result.

Purpose: SC1's results, PAIR-02's source and PAIR-06's verdict. Output: the manifest, the two filled
records, their digests, the tests that pin the outcome, the RESULTS entry.
</objective>
