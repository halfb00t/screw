## Phase Goal

ROADMAP **Goal** (verbatim; not in user-story form, so no story is invented): The owner holds a bolt
and a nut printed from this tool at the default clearance that thread together by hand, and that
default and the printability threshold come from a recorded printed test, not from a forum or vendor
figure.

<objective>
SC3 and PAIR-05, the phase's closing acceptance (D-16): one fresh M8 pair through the shipped default
path, after every code change of the phase. Task 1 (tracer) runs the kernel proof at the default, exports
the pre-registered acceptance pair through the CLI default path and records both. Task 2 is the owner:
the page path, the print, the hand test. Task 3 checks the page files are the shipped default's and
records the result.

Purpose: the goal itself, the owner holding a printed pair that threads by hand. Output: the acceptance
manifest, the RESULTS entry, PAIR-05's evidence.
</objective>
