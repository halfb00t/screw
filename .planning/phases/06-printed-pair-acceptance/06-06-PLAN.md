## Phase Goal

ROADMAP **Goal** (verbatim; not in user-story form, so no story is invented): The owner holds a bolt
and a nut printed from this tool at the default clearance that thread together by hand, and that
default and the printability threshold come from a recorded printed test, not from a forum or vendor
figure.

<objective>
Ship the default the record reads, once and for good. Task 1 is the one-way door: the owner accepts the
reading as permanent or nothing ships. Task 2 (tracer) puts the value into the one parameter model and
proves it end to end: the record re-derives it, and every front end builds a nut without a clearance at
it. Task 3 is the phase's docs commit: L17, the requirement evidence, and the three release triggers.

Purpose: PAIR-02 and SC2; PAIR-06's evidence. Output: the default in `params.py` with its two tests, the
rewritten tests that pinned "required", L17, REQUIREMENTS.md, the debt triggers.
</objective>
