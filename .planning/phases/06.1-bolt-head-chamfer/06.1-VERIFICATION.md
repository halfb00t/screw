### Judgment on the two review warnings

**WR-01 (info document for angles the builder refuses): a WARNING, not a gap in the phase goal.**

What I ran (above): at 1e-5 degrees `derive` prints `chamfer_depth` 1.35e-7, `corner_edge_left` 3.9999998650 and `volume` 804.7065042; the build is refused by the head piece gate (2.14e-7 mm3 against its oracle). At 1e-10 the kernel raises `Standard_Failure`. At 5e-324 `head_chamfer_volume` raises `ValueError` (`thread.py:668-669`), `derive` withholds the volume and warns; the build is refused as `Geometry kernel failed (ValueError)`.

Against L02: the printed rows are the true values of the stated model at that angle, not plausible wrong numbers. A value that cannot be computed honestly (5e-324) already yields a warning and no number. Against D-18: the build outcome required, "built within `T_PIECE` or refused as a named `BuildError` (API 422 `build_error`), never a 500 or a wrong number", is met for every angle I tried. D-18 does not ask the info document to foresee the refusal, and the info document foresees no other kernel refusal either. The sole precedent, the thread-length floor, is a deterministic rule on fields, not a kernel outcome.

What is real: for 1e-5 and below, the band warning says "built as asked (L19)" about a part that cannot be built, the 5e-324 volume warning gives a false reason ("not a finite number" for a closed form that is finite), and the failure text says "Geometry kernel failed" when the kernel never ran (`solid/__init__.py:230-231` raises before it). It is an honesty defect in wording, reachable only by a user typing an angle under about 1e-4 degrees, and the build still refuses it with a 422. I recommend the review's smaller fix, or a `docs/tech_debt/active/` item (severity must) with the owner's sign-off, before the phase is closed. No debt item exists for it today (`docs/tech_debt/active` has no 06.1 entry).

**WR-02 (`assemble(head=None)` fuses the sharp prism): a WARNING, not a gap in the phase goal.**

The product path always passes the head: `_build_bolt` calls `bolt.assemble(p, thread, head)` (`solid/__init__.py:238`), and the kernel check above shows the shipped bolt is chamfered. The goal's clause "inserted before Phase 7 so the re-sweep measures the chamfered head" gives the reason for the phase's place in the order. It is not a deliverable of 06.1, and 06.1-02-PLAN states the `head=None` default and the untouched `bench/` scripts on purpose ("`bench/end_cuts.py`, `bench/head_fuse.py` and their tests are unchanged"). Product-path benches (`bench/build_time.py:105`, `bench/export_cost.py:167`) call `solid.build(p)` and so measure the chamfered head.

What is real: `bench/head_fuse.py` and `bench/end_cuts.py` build `BoltParams` with the default 30 degree chamfer and fuse a sharp head. Their closed forms (`bench/head_fuse.py::closed`, `bolt_closed_volume`) are sharp as well, so nothing fails, but `head_fuse.assemble`'s docstring ("`_build` calls exactly this code ... in the product's order") is now false, and any Phase 7 number from those two probes describes a part the product no longer ships. Phase 7 planning must either pass the chamfered head or label those sweeps sharp-head. Making `head` required, as the review proposes, is the safer shape. I record this as an input to Phase 7, not a failure of 06.1.
