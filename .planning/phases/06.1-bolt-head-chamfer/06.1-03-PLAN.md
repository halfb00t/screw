## Phase Goal

ROADMAP **Goal** (verbatim): A bolt or screw (ISO 4014, ISO 4017) gets a chamfered hex head: one
`head_chamfer` field, an angle in degrees, default 30, where 0 builds the sharp head of today. The
chamfer is a cone from the inscribed circle `s / 2` on the top face only, the same stated model as the
nut face chamfer (L15, R4 a), so it clips the six corners; the bearing face is unchanged. The head's
closed form stays exact for the cone-clipped prism, the field ships on UI, API and CLI with its tests,
and a new decision-log entry supersedes L13 D-01's "no top chamfer".

<objective>
The info document says what was cut. Task 1 (tracer) adds the four D-10 rows when the head is chamfered
and the sharp-head note when it is not, through `derive()` to the API, the CLI and the page. Task 2 adds
the band and cap warnings and rewrites the standing warning (D-12) on all three front ends.

Purpose: L02, a number the tool prints is one someone cuts metal to, so the depth is the depth as cut,
a feature that does not exist gets a note and no zero rows, and every cap and off-band choice is said
out loud. Output: `_bolt` rows, note, warnings and the new standing warning, with their tests.
</objective>
