# Phase 06.1 Plan 01: Head chamfer maths Summary

**Analytic closed form for the volume a 30 degree top-face cone removes from a hex head (pi-part exact, acos part by series below theta 0.05), plus the shared capped-depth predicate, the 15 to 30 band and cap warnings, and the explicit-value conflict text, all kernel-free in `screw.calc.thread`.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** about 30 min (two pre-commit `make verify` runs of about 4 min each)
- **Completed:** 2026-10-24T11:08:27Z
- **Tasks:** 2
- **Files modified:** 3 (340 insertions, no deletions)

## Accomplishments

- `head_chamfer_volume(10.0, 30.0, 1e9)` equals 1.0855309493921927 to rel 1e-12 and agrees with `face_chamfer_volume` and `_polar_chamfer` to rel 1e-6 over s {4, 30} x 5 angles x 4 depth fractions (40 cases), capped and uncapped.
- Stable at the extremes: 89.99999999999999 degrees at depth 2 on s 10 gives 16.1254..., within 1e-6 of the hex-rim identity; depth fractions down to 1e-12 hold the identity to 1e-8; the series seam at theta 0.05 differs by under 1e-8 across a 2e-9 depth step. Unusable input (angle 0, 90, negative, nan, 5e-324; negative or non-finite depth; non-positive or non-finite s) raises `ValueError`, never `ZeroDivisionError`.
- `head_chamfer_depths` is the single predicate; `head_chamfer_warnings` and `head_chamfer_conflict` both call it, so the cap warning and the 422 rule fire at the same boundary (full > k / 2).
- Nothing in `src/` calls the new names yet: the default part is unchanged.
