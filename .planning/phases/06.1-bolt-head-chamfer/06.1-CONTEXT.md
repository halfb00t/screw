# Phase 06.1: Bolt head chamfer - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-10-24
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

A bolt or screw (ISO 4014, ISO 4017) gets a chamfered hex head through one new `BoltParams`
field, `head_chamfer`, an angle in degrees from the top face, default 30, where 0 builds today's
sharp head. The chamfer is a cone from the inscribed circle `s / 2` on the **top face only**
(the same stated model as the nut face chamfer, L15 R4 a), so it clips the six corners; the
bearing face is unchanged. The head's closed form stays exact for the cone-clipped prism, the
field ships on UI, API and CLI with its tests, and a new decision-log entry (next free id,
expected `L19`) supersedes L13 D-01's "no top chamfer".

Fixed by the owner ruling of 2026-10-24 (ROADMAP.md, not re-discussed): field name
`head_chamfer`, unit degrees, default 30, 0 = sharp, top face only, cone start `s / 2`, inserted
before Phase 7 so the re-sweep measures the chamfered head. One-way: every link that omits the
field gets the chamfered head (allowed by L13's reversibility note while the standing warning
says "not a product build").

Not in scope: the nut (its angle stays the constant `CHAMFER_ANGLE_DEG = 30`), a washer face, a
bearing-face chamfer, fine-STL caps and timeouts (Phase 7 re-measures with the chamfered head).

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Angle range and limits
- **D-01:** `head_chamfer` accepts `0 <= beta < 90` degrees, validated once at the model
  boundary (`ge=0`, `lt=90`, finite). 90 and above is a 422 on the field. 0 builds the sharp
  head exactly as today, with no cut and no chamfer volume term.
- **D-02:** An angle in the accepted range but outside the ISO 15 to 30 degree band (any value
  in `(0, 15)` or `(30, 90)`) builds as asked and adds a warning to the info document naming
  `head_chamfer` and the standard's 15 to 30 degree band. 0 is not off-band. It is the sharp head
  and carries no band warning.
- **D-03:** The model's corner depth is `(R - s/2) * tan(beta)` with `R = s / 2 / cos 30°`. The
  allowed depth is `k / 2`, which mirrors the nut's `m / 2` cap (L15). Above the cut, the lower
  half of the head keeps its sharp corners and the bearing face is untouched.
- **D-04:** When the model depth exceeds `k / 2`:
  - **`head_chamfer` explicit** (in the raw API query or the CLI argv, checked after `--preset`
    expansion): a 422 naming `head_chamfer` and `k` (CLI: exit 2, same words, parity-tested like
    `thread_length`). The 422 fires at the same threshold as the cap: `depth > k / 2`. No zone
    exists where an explicit beta builds a part that differs from its model.
  - **`head_chamfer` defaulted** (30): cap the cut at `k / 2` (a cone plus a flat at that depth,
    the shape `face_chamfer_volume` already integrates with its `depth` argument) and warn,
    naming `head_chamfer` and `k`, in the style of the nut's `end_depths` warning.
- **D-05:** The explicit check lives at the request boundary, in the kind's
  `explicit_conflict` rule used by `refuse_explicit_conflict` (`src/screw/app.py`) and by
  `cli._params`, because FastAPI 0.142.2 cannot tell an explicit value from a default inside the
  model (L13 D-25). The rule must read `s` and `k` from the model's defaults when only
  `head_chamfer` is in the query. Preset-filled `s` and `k` count as explicit, the same as
  `thread_length` after expansion. The outcome depends only on whether `head_chamfer` is
  explicit.
- **D-06:** A direct Python `BoltParams(head_chamfer=80, ...)` is not refused. It is capped at
  `k / 2` and warned. The owner extends the D-25 exception to this one rule and no other, and
  the new decision entry records it.
  — **Reversibility:** costly — the 422 type, its `ctx.fields`, and the CLI exit code join the
  published request-boundary contract that every client and the parity test read.

### Closed form exactness
- **D-07:** Derive an **analytic** closed form for the volume one top-face corner chamfer
  removes from the hex prism: the elementary antiderivative of `hex_arc_inside(s, rho) *
  min((rho - s/2) tan beta, depth)` over `[s/2, R]`, covering both the uncapped and the capped
  (`depth = k/2`) cases. The head volume becomes `(sqrt(3)/2) s^2 k - chamfer(s, beta, depth)`,
  and the whole-bolt closed form subtracts the same term. "Exact" in the roadmap is met as
  written, not approximated.
- **D-08:** The existing numeric `face_chamfer_volume` (40 000 midpoints, ~1e-6 rel) and the
  independent polar integral in `tests/test_end_oracles.py` stay as **test oracles** that the
  analytic form must match: across a grid of `s`, beta (including off-band and near the cap),
  and depth, capped and uncapped. The nut keeps its numeric oracle. Moving the nut to the
  analytic form is out of scope.
- **D-09:** The head chamfer cut gets its **own piece gate**: the volume it removed is judged
  against its analytic oracle at `T_PIECE` before the whole bolt is judged at `T_GATE`, like
  each nut cut (P8, D-10), because a wrong or skipped chamfer can hide inside `T_GATE`. It costs
  one more precise volume per build, and Phase 7 measures that cost.

### Info document and warning
- **D-10:** With `head_chamfer > 0`, the HEAD group adds four rows: **chamfer angle** (echo of
  the field, degrees, labelled as a stated model, because the ISO figure prints a 15 to 30
  band and not a number); **corner chamfer depth** as cut,
  `min((R - s/2) tan beta, k/2)`; **chamfer circle diameter** (where the cone meets the top
  face, `= s`); **corner edge left**, `k - depth`. Each row carries its formula string, like the
  existing rows.
- **D-11:** With `head_chamfer = 0`, the four rows are omitted and a note says the head has sharp
  corners (`head_chamfer 0`), following the `NO_FILLET_NOTE` pattern. No zero-valued rows
  appear for a feature that does not exist.
- **D-12:** The standing warning becomes "ends modelled per L15 and L19: bolt tip chamfer, hex
  head chamfer, under-head fillet on an ISO 4014 plain shank, nut lead-in countersinks and face
  chamfers; nominal, not toleranced; not a product build". Use the real id of the new entry if
  it is not L19. The pinned strings in `tests/test_cli.py` and `tests/test_calc.py` change in the
  same commit. The volume row's formula text names the head chamfer term.
  — **Reversibility:** costly — the info document's warning and labels are a published shape
  that every consumer reads (L13).

### Presets and pair
- **D-13:** An ISO preset never touches `head_chamfer`: no row prints an angle (it is a band),
  so the user's value or the default 30 stays (L02).
- **D-14:** The kernel pair proof (`src/screw/solid/pair.py`) builds a rod and a cylindrical
  blank with no head, so the head chamfer does not reach it and nothing changes there.

### Decision log
- **D-15:** A new entry (next free id, expected `L19`) supersedes L13 D-01's "no top chamfer"
  and records D-01 to D-13. It cites the owner ruling of 2026-10-24, the R4 a stated model
  (GOST ISO 4032-2014 Figure 1 footnote b band, 30 degree end) as the source of the default,
  the one-way default change, and the D-25 exception extension (D-06).
  — **Reversibility:** one-way — `head_chamfer`'s name and default 30 become part of every
  shareable link that omits the field. Changing either later changes parts people already
  linked.

### Owner rulings after research (2026-10-24, plan-phase)
- **D-16:** `head_chamfer` is declared **last** in `BoltParams`, so `slug()` keeps the old slug
  as a prefix and gains `_head_chamfer30` at the default. Every default download file name
  changes; the new decision entry records this as a published-shape change (RESEARCH A1).
- **D-17:** D-14 is corrected: `solid/pair.py:269` builds a real `BoltParams` evidence bolt,
  which now gets the default 30 degree chamfer. `pair.py` stays unchanged; one test documents
  that the evidence bolt carries the default `head_chamfer` and still builds, and the new
  decision entry states the correction (RESEARCH A4).
- **D-18:** No angle floor. D-01's range stays `0 <= beta < 90`. A test proves that tiny
  angles either build within `T_PIECE` or are refused as a named `BuildError` (API 422
  `build_error`), never a 500 or a wrong number (RESEARCH A7).
- **D-19:** The D-11 sharp-head note appears on **every** part with `head_chamfer = 0`,
  catalogue match or not; the `PartInfo.notes` field description is reworded to allow a
  build-describing note (RESEARCH A2).

### Claude's Discretion
- Where the cut runs in the bolt pipeline (it must come after the head exists, and its piece
  gate must come before the whole-part gate) and how the cutter is built (a cone overshooting
  the top face, as `nut.face_chamfer` does).
- UI step and placement: the form is generated from the field schema (`group="Head"`,
  `unit="°"` or "deg", a sensible `step`), so this follows the existing `_f` pattern.
- CLI flag `--head-chamfer` follows the existing field-to-flag rule.
- Exact warning wording beyond the facts each warning must name.
- How the analytic integral is organised (one function with a depth cap, or split terms), as
  long as it is pure `math`, lives in `calc/thread.py` and never imports `cadquery`.

</decisions>

<code_context>
## Existing Code Insights

### Reusable Assets
- `face_chamfer_volume(s, r0, angle_deg, depth)`: already models the cone from `r0` with a depth
  cap, so it serves as the numeric cross-check oracle for the analytic form.
- `hex_arc_inside(s, rho)`: the integrand's arc. Its closed form is `rho(2 pi - 12 acos(a/rho))`,
  which integrates analytically.
- `nut.face_chamfer`: the cutter shape to copy for the top face, with an overshoot outside the
  part that removes nothing.
- `explicit_conflict(kind, values)` + `refuse_explicit_conflict`: the existing request-boundary
  hook for an explicit-value 422 with `ctx.fields`.

### Established Patterns
- Cut, then `check_structure`, then the piece gate at `T_PIECE` against its own oracle, then the
  whole-part gate at `T_GATE`.
- Cap-and-warn messages name the field and the decision id (`end_depths`).
- Stated-model constants carry a comment that cites their read and band (`thread.py` R2 to R4
  block).
- Pure maths stays in `calc/`, and its import-boundary contract forbids `cadquery`.

### Integration Points
- `BoltParams` new field → schema-driven UI form, API query model, CLI flag.
- `_build_bolt` → new cut and piece gate. Bolt closed form → subtract the chamfer term.
- Bolt info rows + warnings → D-10 to D-12.
- Tests pinning the default M6 x 20 volume or warning strings → update (the default part changes).

</code_context>

<specifics>
## Specific Ideas

- The cap depth is `k / 2`, chosen explicitly to mirror the nut's `m / 2` and to keep the bearing
  face unchanged, as the roadmap requires.
- At the default M6 x 20 (`s` 10, `k` 4), the 30 degree model depth is about 0.45 mm, far below
  `k / 2` = 2 mm. The cap only binds near beta ≈ 69° at these fields, or with a large `s` and a
  small `k`.

</specifics>

<deferred>
## Deferred Ideas

- The nut face chamfer could move to the analytic closed form too. It changes nothing a user
  sees, so it is not for now.
- A configurable nut chamfer angle (a `NutParams` field) is not requested. The nut stays
  `CHAMFER_ANGLE_DEG = 30`.
- Fine-STL size and timeout impact of the extra cut and the extra precise volume belong to
  Phase 7 (OPER-01, OPER-02).

</deferred>
