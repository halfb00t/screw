## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 8 goal (verbatim, not a user story): Close the gaps the v1.0 milestone audit found so the
first release ships with OPER-03 met: no admitted part ends in a kernel `422 build_error`, every build
path has a deadline or a logged reason it has none, and the front ends answer the same part the same
way.

This plan fits the fine-sweep cost feature and the fine envelope by F10 as F16 amends it (the owner's
S9 ruling, L38) and ships both as constants (D-05) without moving anything (D-02); 08-13 writes L38's
two `ENVELOPE` values and switches admission. The two were one plan, split to keep each inside the
context budget, along the line `make verify` draws: everything that moves an admission lands in 08-13.

Replanned 2026-11-04 after S9 fired on the first fit (F16). The planner's scratch recompute at
replanning (not evidence; the committed judge decides) reproduces L38's two widened values exactly and
predicts that S9 fires again at F16 (ii) after the feature: see Task 1 step 4.

<objective>
Fit the fine-sweep feature (`FINE_COEFFICIENTS`) and `FINE_ENVELOPE` from the Phase 8 runner readings
by F10 and F16 (L38) and ship both through the emit-constants block, with `cost.fits` and `ENVELOPE`
unchanged.

Purpose: the fine sweep costs more time and STEP bytes than the shipped fit predicts, and one envelope
over the shipped prediction wide enough for the worst fine corner refused 84 catalogue and default
parts (S9, L38). The fine parts get a prediction of their own, fitted from the readings by a judge,
not by hand, so admission covers them without refusing the catalogue. Output: the judge, `fine-fit.json`
and its regeneration test, `predict`'s coefficient table, the extended emit block.
</objective>
