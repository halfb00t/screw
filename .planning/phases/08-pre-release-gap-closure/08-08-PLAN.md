## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 8 goal (verbatim, not a user story): Close the gaps the v1.0 milestone audit found so the
first release ships with OPER-03 met: no admitted part ends in a kernel `422 build_error`, every build
path has a deadline or a logged reason it has none, and the front ends answer the same part the same
way.

This plan fits the fine envelope and ships it as a constant (D-05) without moving anything (D-02); 08-13
switches admission to it. The two were one plan, split to keep each inside the context budget, along
the line `make verify` draws: everything that moves an admission lands in 08-13's one commit.

<objective>
Fit `FINE_ENVELOPE` from the Phase 8 runner readings by F10 and ship it through the emit-constants
block, with `cost.fits` unchanged.

Purpose: the fine sweep costs more time and STEP bytes than the shipped fit predicts; admission must
cover it or a fine part could pass its timeout or budget, and the envelope must come from the readings
by a judge, not by hand. Output: the judge, `fine-fit.json` and its regeneration test, the extended
emit block.
</objective>
