## Phase Goal

ROADMAP **Goal** (verbatim; not in user-story form, so the MVP user-story framing is not invented
here): Every configuration a user is allowed to ask for builds inside a per-build timeout measured on
linux/amd64 over the full grid, or is capped and warned or refused with a `422`, and every runtime
bound cites the run that set it.

Spec-less probe fallback applied: the OPER-02 empty edge (a point with no ok reading has no median and
no max) is the seventh truth.

<objective>
Take the first runner figures of the phase, in the pre-registered order: confirm the instrument on the
runner (A2), then run the exploration, spread, walls and controls sets, confirm the walls at 5 reps,
and derive the D-19 margin and shard sizes. Record everything.

Purpose: the walls (07-07), the fit (07-09) and the Runs II sets (07-15) need these numbers. Output: runner
records committed, RESULTS sections, m and shard sizes.

Running time: the dispatched jobs take hours of runner time (explore alone is about 1,900 readings over
40 shards, each reading a fresh container). Poll with `gh run view <id> --json status,conclusion` in
Bash calls of at most 600000 ms; do not hold one call open.
</objective>
