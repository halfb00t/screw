## Trust Boundaries

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-07-15 | Denial of service | contention and drift containers | medium | mitigate | the same `--memory` cap as every reading; a contention reading past 600 s or a drift reading past its own `deadline_s` is `killed` and its processes terminated |
| T-07-16 | Tampering | point fields reaching the container | medium | mitigate | the parent validates every field of the three kinds and passes the point as one argv element (no shell); tested |
| T-07-17 | Tampering | memory figure from a scaled single reading | high | mitigate | every N is its own container reading; no driver multiplies a peak; R16's terms are measured |
| T-07-59 | Tampering | contention slot routing | high | mitigate | every point goes through `executor_for`; the set's computed slot is checked in the container, and a mismatch is `crashed` with no figures, so a reading with two builds on one worker never yields a number |
| T-07-SC | Tampering | package installs | low | accept | no package is installed or added |
</threat_model>
