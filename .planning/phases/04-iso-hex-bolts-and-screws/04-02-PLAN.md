## Phase Goal

"M6 × 20, ISO 4017" (or an ISO 4014 size and length) and gets a hex head bolt or screw whose
explicit millimetre fields were filled from a cited, tested table row, with an info panel whose every
number traces to a field or a row.

<objective>
The owner's WR-02 ruling (D-21): key the fine-STL caps by hand, with the left-hand caps re-derived
from the records by the code's own convention (the D-21 clarification: 72, 77, 78, 75, 91, 91 mm, no
cap for M8 left-hand), and say what was measured — the fine STL plus its gzip copy over the 64 MiB
budget. Runs in wave 1 beside the owner's opening read: it touches no table, no field and no default.

"L11 measured it over the export budget" although L11 measured it inside. Plan 04-04 later moves the
caps' input from `length` to the built thread `t` (D-22); this plan keeps `length`. The decision log
is not touched here: L13 records the hand-keyed rule and L12 gets its factual correction and a
pointer in one commit of plan 04-14, so no entry is amended in place and no pointer dangles.
Output: the re-keyed table, its recomputation test, the reworded refusal and comments, the debt item
resolved, all in one commit.
</objective>
