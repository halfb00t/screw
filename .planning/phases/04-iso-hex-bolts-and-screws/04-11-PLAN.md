## Phase Goal

ROADMAP **Goal** (verbatim; not in user-story form, so no story is invented here): A user picks
"M6 × 20, ISO 4017" (or an ISO 4014 size and length) and gets a hex head bolt or screw whose
explicit millimetre fields were filled from a cited, tested table row, with an info panel whose every
number traces to a field or a row.

<objective>
The info document becomes the traceable panel D-17, D-18 and D-20 describe: rows in groups; on an exact
match the cited row values, each labelled with its source and sense; on no match no row value and one
warning; and two notes, the product grade and, at M10, M12 and M14, the ISO wrench size against the
withdrawn DIN one.

Purpose: SC5 and INFO-03 ("every number traceable to a field or a cited, tested row; no honest value
means a warning and no number") and INFO-05, with the full tables now in place to test against.
Output: the grouped document, the row values, the notes, the tests.
</objective>
