## Phase Goal

ROADMAP **Goal** (verbatim; not in user-story form, so no story is invented here): A user picks
"M6 × 20, ISO 4017" (or an ISO 4014 size and length) and gets a hex head bolt or screw whose
explicit millimetre fields were filled from a cited, tested table row, with an info panel whose every
number traces to a field or a row.

<objective>
ISO 4014:2022 for M2-M20: heads, b by length band, and the printed ls,min / lg,max cells, by the same
double-entry discipline; then every listed ISO 4014 bolt becomes a catalogue row that fills
`thread_length = b` and is recognised on the info document.

Purpose: TYPE-01's table half and THRD-03's "the ISO 4014 preset fills b from the table by length band".
Output: `tables/iso4014.py`, two row types, the 4014 catalogue rows, their tests and owner copy.
Estimate: raw 110000 tokens against the 100000 smart-zone budget (confidence low, uncalibrated); the owner accepted the overage on 2026-10-11 without a split because the task split already marks the seam and the project runs plans sequentially.
</objective>
