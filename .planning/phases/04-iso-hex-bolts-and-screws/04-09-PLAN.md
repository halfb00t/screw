## Phase Goal

ROADMAP **Goal** (verbatim; not in user-story form, so no story is invented here): A user picks
"M6 × 20, ISO 4017" (or an ISO 4014 size and length) and gets a hex head bolt or screw whose
explicit millimetre fields were filled from a cited, tested table row, with an info panel whose every
number traces to a field or a row.

<objective>
Every ISO 262:2023 size and every ISO 4017:2022 row for M2-M20, by the discipline plans 04-07 and 04-08 proved on
M6: the owner's full copy (one stop), the agent's independent transcription, the cell-by-cell diff,
one relation test per row, and the series rules the standard's footnotes give. After this plan every
ISO 4017 screw the standard lists matches its row on the info document.

Purpose: TABL-02 to TABL-05 for ISO 262 and ISO 4017, and TYPE-02's length range to the 200 mm cap.
Output: the full rows, their owner copy, series and cross-table tests, the owner's A1/A3 answers.

Scope: three tasks, no fourth (`verify.plan-structure` reports task_count 3; a raw count of the
opening tag string reads one more because it also matches the tasks wrapper). Tasks 2 and 3 stay two
commits: each double-enters a different body of rows (sizes and heads, then about 200 lengths), and
one commit holding both would be too large to review cell by cell.
Estimate: raw 120000 tokens against the 100000 smart-zone budget (confidence low, uncalibrated); the owner accepted the overage on 2026-10-11 without a split because the task split already marks the seam and the project runs plans sequentially.
</objective>
