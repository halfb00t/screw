## Phase Goal

ROADMAP **Goal** (verbatim; not in user-story form): For any bolt a user can get the matching ISO 4032
nut at a chosen radial clearance, see the pair's numbers on the info panel, and get a kernel verdict on
whether the pair mates, one that cannot report `proven` when its own control did not fire.

<objective>
The bolt's ends: a lead-in tip cone on every bolt and the under-head fillet ring where ISO 4014 has a
plain shank, each gated twice (the whole part at T_GATE, the piece at T_PIECE) with positive controls,
and a watertight sweep. Output: the cited fillet radii, the cut and the ring, the gates, the tests.
</objective>
