## Phase Goal

ROADMAP **Goal** (verbatim; not in user-story form): For any bolt a user can get the matching ISO 4032
nut at a chosen radial clearance, see the pair's numbers on the info panel, and get a kernel verdict on
whether the pair mates, one that cannot report `proven` when its own control did not fire.

<objective>
Ship the pair proof as a solid-package function: the measured rule moves kernel-free into `calc/pair.py`,
the measured bodies into `solid/pair.py`, `prove` assembles them into a report, the shipped-solid evidence
row is printed beside the verdict, and the contract that keeps it off every route lands with it. The CLI
command and the tiers are 05-15. Output: two modules, a contract, the tests.
</objective>
