## Phase Goal

ROADMAP **Goal** (verbatim; not in user-story form): For any bolt a user can get the matching ISO 4032
nut at a chosen radial clearance, see the pair's numbers on the info panel, and get a kernel verdict on
whether the pair mates, one that cannot report `proven` when its own control did not fire.

<objective>
Expose the pair proof where PAIR-04 says: a `screw pair` CLI command with honest exit codes, one cell in
`make verify`, the full grid as a `pair`-marked tier with its own workflow, and the first ubuntu reading
of the verify cell before anything lands. Output: the command, the tiers, the workflow, the draft PR.
</objective>
