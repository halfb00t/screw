## Phase Goal

ROADMAP **Goal** (verbatim; not in user-story form): For any bolt a user can get the matching ISO 4032
nut at a chosen radial clearance, see the pair's numbers on the info panel, and get a kernel verdict on
whether the pair mates, one that cannot report `proven` when its own control did not fire.

THRD-04 gate will compare the chamfered and filleted parts against, their validity conditions, the end
models the owner ruled on, the capped depths, and the differential tolerance. Kernel-free, so they run in
`make verify` on every keystroke path. Output: oracle functions and constants, their tests, the bench
import-back.
</objective>
