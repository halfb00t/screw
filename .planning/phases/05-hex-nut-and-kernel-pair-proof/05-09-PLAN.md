## Phase Goal

ROADMAP **Goal** (verbatim; not in user-story form): For any bolt a user can get the matching ISO 4032
nut at a chosen radial clearance, see the pair's numbers on the info panel, and get a kernel verdict on
whether the pair mates, one that cannot report `proven` when its own control did not fire.

<objective>
The panel: the nominal pitch and minor diameters on both kinds, the bought-part limits under INFO-02's
heading on an exact size, the nut's pair numbers in fixed order, and the M18 label from the cited record.
Every number is kernel-free. Output: three calc changes and their tests.
</objective>
