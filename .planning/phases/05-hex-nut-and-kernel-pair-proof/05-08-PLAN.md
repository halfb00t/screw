## Phase Goal

ROADMAP **Goal** (verbatim; not in user-story form): For any bolt a user can get the matching ISO 4032
nut at a chosen radial clearance, see the pair's numbers on the info panel, and get a kernel verdict on
whether the pair mates, one that cannot report `proven` when its own control did not fire.

<objective>
Ship the numbers the panel's thread rows and limits group read, from the GOST texts L14 maps: the GOST
24705-2004 (ISO 724:1993, MOD) basic diameters and the GOST 16093-2004 deviations and tolerances, each
double-entered, the 6g and 6H limits derived from them by exact decimal addition of printed values (no
GOST here prints ISO 965-2's limit tables), and the ISO 68-1 basic-profile formulas the panel prints
(R13). Output: rows, two formulas, two owner copies, the diffs, the derivation and relation tests. The
panel shows them in 05-09.
</objective>
