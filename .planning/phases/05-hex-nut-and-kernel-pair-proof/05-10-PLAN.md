## Phase Goal

ROADMAP **Goal** (verbatim; not in user-story form): For any bolt a user can get the matching ISO 4032
nut at a chosen radial clearance, see the pair's numbers on the info panel, and get a kernel verdict on
whether the pair mates, one that cannot report `proven` when its own control did not fire.

<objective>
The matching part: one `counterpart` key on every info document, printed alike by the API and the CLI,
and one generic link on the page; plus the page's two traps with a defaultless field (P9) and the UI
contract amendments for everything Phase 5 put on the page. Output: the model, the link, the record.
</objective>
