## Phase Goal

ROADMAP **Goal** (verbatim; not in user-story form): For any bolt a user can get the matching ISO 4032
nut at a chosen radial clearance, see the pair's numbers on the info panel, and get a kernel verdict on
whether the pair mates, one that cannot report `proven` when its own control did not fire.

<objective>
The nut's refusals and its clearance warning: the clearance-aware root radius (D-04), the thin-nut rule
(D-13), the turn cap on the void the builder makes (P11), the fine-STL rule (R12), the non-positive
clearance warning, and kernel evidence that a positive clearance moves the nut's surfaces outward on both
hands (PAIR-01). Output: three validators, four refusal texts, one warning, their tests.
</objective>
